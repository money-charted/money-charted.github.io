Money Charted

Money, explained with real data and clear charts.

Money Charted is a YouTube channel that explains personal finance and the economy with charts built from official data: inflation, interest rates, savings, pensions, energy bills and markets, for viewers in the UK, the US and around the world. Every video lists its sources in the description.

Money Charted Publisher

Money Charted Publisher is our own internal tool. After we review and approve a video, it uploads it to our YouTube channel with its title, description, thumbnail, subtitles and publish time, adds it to a playlist, and posts a comment. It is used only with our own channel. See the privacy policy for what it accesses and stores.
