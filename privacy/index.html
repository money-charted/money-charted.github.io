Money Charted Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Money Charted Publisher is a private tool used by Money Charted, operated by Iqra ("we") to upload videos to our own YouTube channel, Money Charted. It uses YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public and is used only with our own channel.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube data is handled in line with the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the tool:

  • uploads videos, thumbnails and subtitles to our own channel, and sets their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times;
  • adds our videos to our own playlists, and posts a comment on our own videos;
  • reads the basic details of our own channel and videos (channel name, video IDs, whether a video is public) to confirm uploads.

It does not access any other person's channel, videos, comments or personal data, and does not access YouTube analytics, subscribers' details or viewing data.

What is stored, and where

  • Sign-in token: the access token Google issues is stored only on the computer that runs the tool, so it can upload without signing in each time.
  • Upload records: the IDs, links, titles and publish times of our own uploaded videos, stored locally in the tool's database.

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from YouTube is stored on any server other than the local computer.

Keeping and deleting data

Upload records and the sign-in token are kept only while the tool is in use with our channel. If access is revoked, or we stop using the tool, the stored token and YouTube data are deleted within 7 days. Deleting the tool's data folder removes everything it has stored.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions (choose "Money Charted Publisher", then "Remove access").
